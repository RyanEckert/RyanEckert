### 🪸 About Me

I'm a coral reef ecologist and population genomicist at **FAU Harbor Branch Oceanographic Institute**. My research focuses on mesophotic coral ecosystems, cryptic species diversity, and depth-stratified connectivity throughout the Florida Reef Tract. Gulf of Mexico, and Caribbean. I use genomic toolss to understand how reef populations are structured across depth and space.

Before my PhD, I spent eight years as a research specialist at **NOAA's Flower Garden Banks National Marine Sanctuary**. I also serve as an editor at [Mesophotic.org](https://mesophotic.org).
